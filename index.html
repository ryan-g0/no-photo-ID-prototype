Design notes

• Adaptive: next question chosen by information gain over current candidates — never asks what it can't split.
• Front-door gate (Stage 0): opens with bite / spit / just-saw. On a bite: one early red-flag escalation (act-now), then the sighting leads; the three syndrome questions run guaranteed but salience-ordered.
• Safety asymmetry & no de-escalation: a reported bite always shows the emergency banner regardless of the top species; "no symptoms yet" stays non-diagnostic.
• ⚠ Decision-support, not diagnosis: the syndrome layer needs clinical sign-off before real use.
